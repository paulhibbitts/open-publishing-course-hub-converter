Grav Open Publishing Space → Docsify-This or Starter Kit (Docsify → Grav is coming next)

Upload the pages of a Grav Open Publishing Space site and get its guides back as Docsify Markdown pages, laid out like the Docsify-This Multiple Guides Site and Guide Site templates: a Guides page, each guide's home with its section cards, its sections and pages, and a sidebar with the reading order.

The same pages work in a repo shown by Docsify-This and in a Docsify Starter Kit's docs/ folder. Only guides are converted: blog posts and other pages aren't included.

For a site with one guide, like the Guide Site template: the guide's home becomes the site's home page, with no Guides page. If the zip has several guides, the first one is used.
What exactly does this do?

Each guide (a Section List page) becomes a folder with its home page, a folder per section with its pages, a _sidebar.md with the reading order (which also sets Previous/Next) and a _navbar.md. The Guides List page becomes home.md, with a card for each guide.

Callout shortcodes such as [reflection] become Docsify callouts, Learning Objectives appear at the top of each section, links between pages are adjusted, and images are copied beside their pages. Your own text is otherwise left as it is.

Each page also gets a hidden note with its original Grav settings. Docsify doesn't show it, and it will let the pages go back to Grav unchanged once the Docsify → Grav direction is ready.

Uploaded files are processed in a temporary folder and deleted immediately after conversion. Nothing is stored on the server.
