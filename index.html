What exactly does this do?

Docsify to Grav: the repo is downloaded from GitHub. Each guide's sidebar gives its sections and pages, in order; its pages give their titles, section numbers and Learning Objectives; and the guide home and Guides page give the cards, the Side Box and the authors line. The result is an Open Publishing Space pages folder: a Guides List page, and for each guide a Section List page with numbered section and page folders. Settings only Grav has get the Open Publishing Space defaults. Callouts become shortcodes where their type and heading match one; others stay as callouts, which Open Publishing Space also shows. Pages this converter made go back exactly as they were.

Courses: a course's modules become Docsify pages laid out like the Docsify Open Course Starter Kit – the modules and LMS links in the sidebar, the menu pages and Contact in the navbar, and the home page with its announcement. The way back gives Open Course Hub pages: a module for each sidebar link (module pages not in the sidebar become hidden modules), a menu page for each navbar link, and the sidebar from the Contact page and LMS links. Slides, videos and other embeds become Open Course Hub's shortcodes. Courses this converter made go back exactly as they were.

Grav to Docsify: each guide becomes a folder with its home page, a folder per section with its pages, a _sidebar.md with the reading order (which also sets Previous/Next) and a _navbar.md. The Guides List page becomes home.md, with a card for each guide. Callout shortcodes such as [reflection] become Docsify callouts, Learning Objectives appear at the top of each section, links between pages are adjusted, and images are copied beside their pages. Each page also gets a hidden note with its original Grav settings, which Docsify doesn't show.

Your own text is otherwise left as it is. A conversion-notes.txt in each zip lists what was converted and anything to check.

Repos and uploads are processed in a temporary folder and deleted immediately after conversion. Nothing is stored on the server.
